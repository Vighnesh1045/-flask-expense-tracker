Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 13, 2026

1. Data We Collect

When you use Spendly, we collect the account information you provide (such as your name and email address) and the expense data you enter — amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions. This information is collected solely to operate the Service.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used to provide core Spendly features: storing your expenses, generating spending summaries, and letting you sign in to your account. We do not sell your personal or financial data to third parties.

3. Data Storage

We take reasonable measures to protect your data from unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly does not sell or share your personal or financial data with third parties for marketing purposes. We may use third-party service providers (such as hosting or analytics providers) solely to operate and maintain the Service, and only to the extent necessary for them to perform those functions.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, wish to access, update, or delete your account data, you can reach us at support@spendly.app.
